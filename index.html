About SAMYAK 2026

SAMYAK 2026 is a national-level techno-management fest organized by the Department of MCA. The fest brings students together through technical competitions, creative problem-solving and hands-on learning experiences across modern technology domains.

From prompt engineering and web design to cybersecurity, coding and applied AI, every track is an opportunity to think critically, build confidently and compete fairly.

Fair play

Common rules

Please review these guidelines before registering. Competition-specific instructions from the organizers and judges will also apply.

✓

Complete registration before the announced deadline.

✓

Follow the team-size limits specified for your selected competition.

✓

Work within the time limit announced for the competition.

✓

Submit original work. Plagiarism, copying or misrepresenting another team's work is not allowed.

✓

Internet access and AI-tool availability will be announced separately for each competition.

✓

Submit all required work before the stated deadline.

✓

Judges will verify scores and evaluate submissions according to the announced criteria.

✓

Ties will be resolved according to the rules defined for the respective competition.

✓

Respect fellow participants, organizers, mentors and the decisions of the judging panel.
